# Vietnamese Style Guide — bismliving.com

**Status:** Draft v1. Locked vocabulary + register rules for all Vietnamese copy on the site. Source: verified against Harbor RC, Frank D. Lanterman RC, NLACRC, RCOC, VMRC, GGRC, DDS Early Start, Disability Rights California (RULA), CDSS, CANHR, and Vietnamese-American disability advocacy orgs (VPDCA).

## Register — the single highest-stakes call

- **`quý vị`** for second person. Never `bạn`. (GGRC uses `bạn` — outlier and off-register for older parents; do not copy.)
- **`con em quý vị`** to refer to the adult son/daughter (your child). VN family language doesn't shift when the child becomes an adult. DDS uses this even for adult clients; we follow.
- **`chúng tôi`** for provider voice (we). Never `Bism mình` or similar cutesiness.
- **Southern Vietnamese.** California VN community is overwhelmingly post-1975 South VN diaspora. `ba/má` (Southern) — never `bố/mẹ` (Northern). Neutral `cha mẹ` is safe. `xe hơi` not `ô tô`.
- **`khuyết tật`** is the accepted clinical/official term — use it. **`tàn tật`** is banned (pejorative).
- **No religious framing.** Buddhist/Catholic framing reads as pandering or assuming family's faith. Stay secular-warm.
- **Drop `Kính thưa quý vị`** as a page opener — that's a formal-letter register, not homepage register.

## Terms with NO standard Vietnamese — DROP or paraphrase

- **`person-centered planning`** — no clean VN term. **Don't calque** `lấy người làm trung tâm` (AI/government register). Paraphrase as `kế hoạch riêng cho từng người` or `kế hoạch xoay quanh nhu cầu và mong muốn của cá nhân`.
- **`trauma-informed care`** — no community recognition; even English-side families don't ask for it. **Drop the phrase entirely** on a small-agency consumer site.
- **`neurodivergent`** — no VN equivalent that lands. Older parents won't recognize the concept. **Drop entirely.** Use `khuyết tật phát triển` framing instead.

## Acronyms

**Introduce in English + VN gloss + acronym on first mention; use English acronym alone after.**

> Example: *"Kế Hoạch Chương Trình Cá Nhân (Individual Program Plan, hoặc IPP). IPP của con em quý vị do điều phối viên dịch vụ giúp soạn thảo."*

**Acronyms to keep English on second mention:** IPP, IHSS, ASD, ABA, SSI, Medi-Cal, DDS, ILC.

**Acronyms NOT to lean on (community doesn't recognize them):** SLS, ILS, DSP. Translate fully each time, or pair with VN phrase.

## Cultural framing — what to address head-on

1. **Filial duty tension** — families worry SLS "replaces" family care. Reframe explicitly: *"Gia đình vẫn là trung tâm. Chúng tôi chỉ là người hỗ trợ thêm khi quý vị cần."*
2. **Language barrier fear** — plaster *"Nhân viên nói tiếng Việt"* prominently. Easiest trust win on the site.
3. **Stigma** — do NOT name shame ("There's no shame in asking for help" reads as accusatory). Normalize SLS as *"sự hỗ trợ thêm để con em quý vị sống tự lập và an toàn"*.
4. **Trust signals priority order:** (a) VN founder name visible, (b) FDLRC vendoring `Được Trung tâm Khu vực Frank D. Lanterman cấp giấy phép, mã số nhà cung cấp PD4210`, (c) years of experience, (d) Vietnamese DSP staff + local Glendale/LA address.

## Tonal anchors (phrases to use)

These are pulled from real RC/DDS Vietnamese copy. Echo this style:

- `đồng hành cùng` — walk alongside (parallel to Spanish `acompañar`)
- `con em quý vị` — your child (even when adult)
- `bằng tiếng Việt` — in Vietnamese
- `miễn phí với khách hàng của Trung tâm khu vực` — free for RC clients
- `Dạ` — Southern respectful particle; great in testimonials

## AI-translation tells — DO NOT SHIP

Reject any draft containing any of these.

1. `Dịch vụ cuộc sống có hỗ trợ` — wrong word order. Use `Dịch Vụ Sống Có Hỗ Trợ`. **#1 tell.**
2. `bạn` anywhere → `quý vị`.
3. `tàn tật` → `khuyết tật`.
4. `nhà bán hàng` for vendor → `nhà cung cấp dịch vụ`.
5. `lấy người làm trung tâm` (calque) → paraphrase per glossary.
6. `Kính thưa quý vị` as page opener — formal-letter register, not homepage register.
7. Stripped diacritics on capitalized headlines (`DICH VU` instead of `DỊCH VỤ`).
8. `bố/mẹ` (Northern) → `ba/má` or `cha mẹ`.
9. `khuyết tật trí thông minh` for intellectual disability → `khuyết tật trí tuệ`.
10. Pronoun thrash — mixing `bạn` and `quý vị` on the same page is the dead giveaway of stitched-together AI output.
11. Anglicized word order: `24/7 chăm sóc` → `chăm sóc 24/7` or `suốt 24 giờ, 7 ngày một tuần`.

## Typography & formatting

- **Tonal marks on capitalized headlines must be preserved.** `DỊCH VỤ` not `DICH VU`. Verify after every CMS paste.
- Spot-check every `ư`, `ơ`, `ê`, `ă`, `â`, `đ` against the source.
- Compound nouns: verify word order. `chăm sóc 24/7` not `24/7 chăm sóc`.

## Native review

Before any Vietnamese copy ships:

1. Grep the file against every "AI tell" above. Zero matches.
2. Have a Vietnamese-American native speaker (ideally OC/SGV, age 35+, with health/disability/social-services exposure) read it as a parent looking for SLS for their adult son. Brief: *"Flag any wrong diacritics, wrong pronouns, and any place where the dialect sounds Northern or formal-government."*
